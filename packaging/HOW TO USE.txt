WHATSAPP TRANSFER STALL FINDER - HOW TO USE
===========================================

What this is for
----------------
You are moving WhatsApp from an Android phone to an iPhone and it stops at the
same percentage every time with "An unknown error occurred". One broken message
is usually the cause. This app finds it so you can delete it.

It only reads your backup. It never changes it, and nothing is uploaded.


STEP 1 - Make a backup on the Android phone
-------------------------------------------
1. Open WhatsApp. Tap the three dots > Settings > Chats > Chat backup.
2. Tap "End-to-end encrypted backup" and turn it on.
3. Choose "Use 64-digit encryption key" (NOT a password).
4. WhatsApp shows a long key. Write it down exactly or take a photo of it.
5. Go back and tap "Back up". Wait until it finishes.


STEP 2 - Copy the backup file to your computer
----------------------------------------------
1. Connect the phone to the computer with a USB cable.
   On the phone, pull down the notification bar, tap the USB notification and
   choose "File transfer".
   (On a Mac you need the free "Android File Transfer" app or similar.)
2. On the phone's storage, open these folders in turn:
     Android > media > com.whatsapp > WhatsApp > Databases
3. Copy the file named   msgstore.db.crypt15   to your Desktop.


STEP 3 - Open this app
----------------------
WINDOWS: double-click "WhatsApp Stall Finder.exe".
  If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
  "Run anyway". This appears because the app is not from a big company.

MAC: double-click "WhatsApp Stall Finder".
  If the Mac says it cannot be opened: open System Settings > Privacy &
  Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to the app's name.


STEP 4 - Find the problem
-------------------------
1. Click "Choose file" and pick the msgstore.db.crypt15 file on your Desktop.
2. Type the 64-digit key from Step 1. Spaces do not matter.
3. If you know the percentage where the transfer stops, type it in.
4. Click "Find the problem messages" and wait.

The app then tells you which messages to delete, in which chat, on what date,
and which messages sit right before and after each one so you can find it.


STEP 5 - Delete and try again
-----------------------------
1. On the Android phone, delete the messages in the order listed.
2. Try the transfer to the iPhone again.
3. If it stops at a DIFFERENT percentage, that is good news: one problem is
   fixed and there is another. Repeat from Step 1 with a new backup.


Keep it private
---------------
The backup file, the key, and the "WhatsApp transfer report" folder on your
Desktop contain your messages. Do not send them to anyone, and delete the
report folder and the copied backup file when you are finished.

Deleting a message in WhatsApp cannot be undone.
